## Primary

**★★★★★ 5.0**
**Project:** Custom design and estimating software for a pool construction company
**Hired for:** Software development

I build pools. I am not a software guy. What I wanted was pretty simple in my head: get me off the Windows machine I keep in the corner of my office purely to run my design program, and let me draw on my Mac like a normal person.

That is what I asked for. What he built is something I did not know I was allowed to ask for.

Here is the thing about our industry. The design software everybody uses makes gorgeous 3D, and it does not know what a single dollar costs. So you draw the pool, then you go open a spreadsheet and build the price by hand, then you retype it all into a proposal in Word. Three tools, three chances to get it wrong, and every time the homeowner says "what if the pool was a little bigger" you start the whole loop over. It took me four or five days to get a real number back to somebody.

He listened to all that and then asked the question that changed the project. He asked why the drawing and the price were two separate things at all. Now when I widen the pool, the number moves while I am dragging it. There is a little line in the corner that says something like widening to 16 feet adds $3,160. I sat in a kitchen last month, made three changes the wife asked for, and she signed before I left the driveway. That never happened before, not once in fourteen years.

Some other things he put in that I never requested and now cannot work without. It flags setback problems against the property line while I am drawing, so I am not finding out at permit that I am 7 feet off. There is a sun slider that shows where the shade falls at different times of day, which sells more pools than any render I have ever handed anyone. And I can send the customer a link they open on their phone, spin it around, and pick their own coping and tile with the price moving as they do it.

He helped me create the most creative version of what I wanted, and it was awesome. Fast responses, took the time to actually learn how this trade works instead of guessing, and patient when I described things badly. This did not just save me time, it changed how I sell. Hire him.
